Digital Transformation In Real Time

Using the power of virtual production to drive digital transformation

Innovate UK Immersive Tech Network and Imagination was joined by industry experts for a series of panel discussions and thought-leadership pieces exploring the impact of virtual production and real time content production, each from a different perspective. In this session, we explored utilising Virtual Production to help enable Digital Transformation in real time, including what is required to embrace realtime technologies and workflows into your organisation.

Client: Innovate UK Immersive Tech Network

"Virtual Production can be more accurately described as real time content production" Virtual Production can be more accurately described as real time content production; The process by which digital objects and environments are generated in real time using video games engines such as Unreal and Unity. The same technology that immerses you in living, breathing, photorealistic gaming worlds, is now being used by filmmakers, storytellers, marketers and even product designers, opening up limitless creative possibilities.

About the Series This series aims to demystify the technology and dig into the practical applications, and structural, business and cultural impact of this incredible new world, which is and will continue to transform industries in much the same way that cloud computing and the Internet before it.
