The Future of Experiences

The experience platform that redefined the art of the possible for live experiences.

Over the past decade, experience design has undergone a seismic transformation. The way brands connect with their customers - whether in-person or online, has fundamentally changed, with technology driving this evolution.

Client: Imagination

Tags: Fractional CTO & Technology Strategy, Infrastructure Design & Build, Innovation & Commercialisation, Technology Sourcing & Strategic Partnerships, Technical Due Diligence, Technology Sourcing & Strategic Partnerships

Forged In The Fire Of Live Experiences As Group CTO of Imagination, Anton Christodoulou identified a critical challenge: the need for a more efficient, flexible, and integrated approach to delivering live experiences. Setting out the vision, he spearheaded the design and development of XPKit - transforming a complex problem into a groundbreaking solution. Built using decades of in-house knowledge, XPKit redefined how brands create, scale, and analyse experiences across physical, digital, and virtual spaces, setting a new standard for the industry.

International experience design agency Imagination faced significant challenges in this shifting landscape. Off-the-shelf software lacked the flexibility needed to create bespoke live experiences for product launches, brand activations, exhibitions, music and sports events, location-based experiences, innovation centers, and retail spaces. The disjointed and rigid systems made integration and analysis nearly impossible. Building custom back-end software for every project was time-consuming, expensive, and inherently unreliable.

Enter XPKit.

Born from the demanding world of live experience delivery, XPKit has evolved into a robust, standalone platform. Global brands including Ford, MLB, JLR, Canon, and Visa rely on XPKit to create seamless, groundbreaking experiences for their audiences.

The ultimate integrated solution, XPKit revolutionised technical development, analytics and insights - empowering brands to deliver and scale bold, groundbreaking creative ideas with seamless, complex customer journeys - faster and more efficiently than ever before.
